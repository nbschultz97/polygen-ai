## 5 Key Features to Highlight

### Feature 1: Natural Language to 3D
**Title:** Text-to-3D Generation
**Description:** Describe any object in plain English and get a complete 3D model. "A phone stand with cable routing and adjustable angle" becomes reality in seconds.

### Feature 2: OpenSCAD Code Output
**Title:** Clean, Editable Code
**Description:** Unlike black-box AI, PolyGen outputs readable OpenSCAD code. Modify parameters, learn 3D modeling concepts, and maintain full control over your designs.

### Feature 3: Parametric Design
**Title:** Parametric Customization
**Description:** Every generated model includes adjustable parameters. Change dimensions, tolerances, and features without regenerating. Perfect for iterative prototyping.

### Feature 4: 3D Print Ready
**Title:** Instant STL Export
**Description:** One-click export to STL format. Models are automatically optimized for 3D printing with proper manifold geometry and printability checks.

### Feature 5: Design Library
**Title:** Save & Share Library
**Description:** Build your personal library of AI-generated designs. Share with the community, fork others' creations, and collaborate on complex projects.

# 6. HUNTER RECOMMENDATIONS

## What is a Hunter?

A "hunter" is someone who submits your product to Product Hunt. Having a hunter with a strong following can give you initial visibility and credibility.

## Types of Hunters to Reach Out To

### Tier 1: High-Profile Hunters (5,000+ followers)
**Best for:** Maximum initial visibility
**Examples:**
- Chris Messina (@chrismessina) - 50K+ followers
- Mubashar Iqbal (@mubashariqbal) - Active hunter
- Ben Tossell (@bentossell) - No-code/tools focus
- Kevin William David - Prolific hunter

**Approach:** Requires strong product, warm intro preferred

### Tier 2: Niche-Relevant Hunters (1,000-5,000 followers)
**Best for PolyGen AI:**
- Hunters who focus on AI/ML tools
- Hunters interested in developer tools
- Hunters who cover maker/hardware space
- Hunters active in 3D printing community

**How to find them:**
1. Search Product Hunt for similar products
2. Note who hunted successful AI tools
3. Check their Twitter for interests
4. Look for hunters who also create similar products

### Tier 3: Engaged Community Hunters (500-1,000 followers)
**Best for:** More personalized attention, responsive engagement
**Benefits:** Will likely comment and engage throughout the day

### Self-Hunting Option
**When to self-hunt:**
- You have 500+ Product Hunt followers
- You're an established maker
- You want full control
- You've built an email list specifically for this

**Benefits of self-hunting:**
- Post your own maker comment immediately
- No coordination required
- Full control of timing
- Authentic founder story
